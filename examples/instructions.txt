Load assembly.ino onto your robot BEFORE screwing in the servo horns, assembly.ino will set all angles of servos to 90 degrees so you can screw them in properly. 

Use stand.ino to test that all of your servos have been installed correctly.

Lastly, do not attempt to run code on the robot without plugging in the battery or attaching it to a power supply at 7v first (battery may need to be charged with use of a power supply)
